Opportunities at OSiV

We are preparing to recruit our first generation of members. Students and young researchers from any university can apply, and no prior publication is required.

Application materials

  1. CV.pdf
  2. a short statement (one page) on what you want to work on and why
  3. optional: links to prior projects, code, writing or publications
Our current research directions
  • technology and engineering (AI, computer vision, memory for AI agents, intelligent transportation, robotics)
  • economics, culture and society (innovation and digital transformation, public policy, cultural heritage, sustainable agriculture and tourism)
