A digital map of intellectual property assets in Dong Thap

geographical indications and collective trademarks online, linked with production and traceability data

Status
In preparation
Partner
Dong Thap province
illustrative photo

Overview

The new Dong Thap province (merged with Tien Giang in 2025) has about 135,000 ha of fruit trees and around 2.5 million tonnes of fruit a year; about 49,490 ha already carry 2,715 export growing-area codes.

Its geographical indications and collective trademarks are an underused asset because their legal data, geography, producers and output are scattered across agencies.

Goals

  • Survey, inventory and standardise data on geographical indications and collective trademarks: legal status, right holders, managers, products, areas and users.
  • Build a spatial database and an online map that displays the protected areas.
  • Integrate production volume and traceability data.
  • Support management, updating, search and promotion of these assets.

Approach

  1. Phase 1: survey, baseline, data dictionary, system architecture and data flows.
  2. Phase 2 (planned April to September 2027): standardise data, build the digital map and a pilot online system.
  3. Later phases: integration, testing with agencies and handover.

Expected outcomes

  • A data dictionary and database of intellectual property assets.
  • A public online map of geographical indications and collective trademarks.
  • Integration with production and traceability data.
