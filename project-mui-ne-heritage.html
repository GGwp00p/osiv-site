AI and multimodal interaction for a gamified heritage tour at Po Sah Inu, Mui Ne

a smart welcome kiosk, a multilingual voice guide and a game-like route for green and smart tourism

Status
Ongoing · proposal
Partner
Lam Dong province
illustrative photo

Overview

Visitors at the Po Sah Inu Cham towers near Mui Ne mostly meet static signs and QR codes. The project upgrades this to two-way, interactive heritage interpretation that keeps visitors longer and gives the site management a sustainable smart-destination tool.

Goals

  • Build a validated knowledge base on the Cham towers and the Kate festival, reviewed by cultural experts, as the only source for the AI (80 to 100 topics, questions and answers in three languages).
  • Design an outdoor interactive kiosk: proximity sensors greet visitors and a step pad shows a dynamic QR code; weatherproof and with no biometric data collected.
  • Develop a real-time voice assistant in Vietnamese, English and Russian (speech recognition, retrieval-grounded answers, speech synthesis) that runs in the phone browser without installing an app.
  • Design a gamified heritage route with five touchpoints around the site.

Approach

  1. Survey of current interpretation at the site and digitisation of historical and cultural sources.
  2. Vector database of verified content for retrieval-augmented answers, to avoid wrong or invented cultural information.
  3. Hardware prototyping of the kiosk, then pilot operation with visitors and evaluation.

Expected outcomes

  • A validated heritage knowledge base and multilingual question set.
  • A working kiosk prototype and voice guide.
  • A gamified tour route handed over to the site management.
