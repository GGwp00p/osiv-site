Economics, Culture & Society

We use data and AI to help Vietnamese localities turn what they already have, their farm produce, crafts, heritage, landscapes and water, into lasting value, and we study how firms and policies make that happen.

Most of this work starts from a concrete request of a province or ward and becomes an applied project prepared with the provincial Department of Science and Technology. Our papers then examine the mechanisms behind it: how policy support turns into productivity, how tacit knowledge is transferred, and why firms adopt technology.

Our approach is data first: survey and standardise what exists, measure a baseline, run a pilot with the people who will use it, and only then add AI.

Research themes

Theme 1

Digital governance of agricultural value chains

How can cooperatives and provinces know their supply, prove its origin and sell it at a fair price?

Digital raw-material zones for mango and pineapple, farm logbooks, batch traceability, supply–demand forecasting and a digital map of geographical indications.

  • Smart governance of the mango value chain in An Giang
  • Digital management of mango-growing zones in Dong Thap
  • Digital management of pineapple-growing zones in Dong Thap
  • A digital map of intellectual property assets in Dong Thap
  • Commercializing Intellectual Property in the Agricultural Value Chain of Dong Thap
Theme 2

Tacit knowledge, heritage and AI

How can knowledge that lives in artisans' hands be captured, verified and taught with AI?

Yellow-apricot craft in An Nhon Dong, absorptive capacity in ornamental-plant value chains, and digital heritage as public infrastructure.

  • AI for the indigenous knowledge of yellow-apricot growing in An Nhon Dong
  • The Potential of Digitizing Ornamental Yellow Apricot: Lessons from Research on Bonsai, Heritage Landscapes and Artisan Knowledge
  • Developing Technology Absorptive Capacity through Tacit Knowledge Transfer: A Case Study of the An Nhon Dong Ornamental Plant Value Chain
  • Tacit Knowledge Transfer and Digitalized Absorptive Capacity in the An Nhon Dong Ornamental Plant Value Chain: An Education and Capability-Development Approach
  • From Peripheral Heritage to Public Infrastructure: State-Led Digital Heritage Transformation and Public Value in Soc Bom Bo, Vietnam
Theme 3

Smart and green tourism

What makes a destination such as Mui Ne both greener and smarter, and how should it be governed?

Destination governance and digital transformation for Mui Ne, and an interactive, multilingual heritage tour at Po Sah Inu.

  • AI and multimodal interaction for a gamified heritage tour at Po Sah Inu, Mui Ne
  • From Strategy to Action: Solutions, Implementation Mechanisms and Impact Assessment for Developing Mui Ne into a Green and Smart Tourism Destination
  • Developing Mui Ne into a Green and Smart Tourism Destination: The Role of Innovation, Digital Transformation and Destination Governance
Theme 4

Innovation, policy and firm behaviour

Do firms adopt AI and new technology for value or for legitimacy, and does policy support raise productivity?

Symbolic AI adoption in Southeast Asian firms, policy support and productivity in Dong Thap, and the trade effects of the EU carbon border adjustment mechanism.

  • Competitive Legitimacy Pressure and Symbolic AI Adoption in Emerging-Market Firms: A Conceptual Framework with Illustrative Evidence from Southeast Asia
  • From Policy Support to Productivity: The Mediating Role of Technological Innovation and Management Capability in Dong Thap Firms
  • From Policy Support to Productivity: The Role of Technological Innovation and Management Capability
  • The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism and Trade: Early Evidence from the First Months of the Definitive Regime
Theme 5

Water, energy and resources

How can farms and industries use local resources better under a changing climate?

Rainwater coordination for farming in B'Lao, spent coffee grounds as solid biofuel, and AI robots for scrap sorting.

  • Rainwater coordination for sustainable agriculture in B'Lao
  • Energy Feasibility Boundaries for Converting Spent Coffee Grounds into Solid Biofuel: Effects of Mechanical Dewatering, Drying, and Solids Loss
  • AI-Integrated Robots for Industrial Scrap Sorting in Vietnam
