Digital management of mango-growing zones in Dong Thap

Cat Chu mango, Cao Lanh: production management, batch traceability and value-chain linkage

Status
Completed · proposal submitted, awaiting decision
Partner
Department of Science and Technology, Dong Thap
illustrative photo

Overview

A pilot model for managing the raw-material zone of Cat Chu mango, with the cooperative as the hub that creates, checks and uses data for purchasing, packing, processing, distribution and export.

Goals

  • Standardise production data and track the season.
  • Forecast supply capacity and control quality.
  • Trace products by batch, with data that can be exchanged with the traceability system chosen by the authorities.

Approach

  1. Survey of current zone management and the data needs of the mango chain, with a measured baseline for before-and-after comparison.
  2. A data dictionary, system architecture, business processes and data-exchange plan.
  3. Pilot with cooperatives, firms and households, then evaluation.

Expected outcomes

  • Survey and baseline reports, data dictionary and system design.
  • A pilot digital zone-management model for mango.
