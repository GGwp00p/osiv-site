Digital management of pineapple-growing zones in Dong Thap

data by household, plot, variety, crop cycle and harvest lot

Status
Completed · proposal submitted, awaiting decision
Partner
Department of Science and Technology, Dong Thap
illustrative photo

Overview

The same model for pineapple: the cooperative coordinates the data, which is standardised by household, plot, variety, crop cycle and harvest lot to follow production, forecast supply, control quality and prepare traceability records. Tan Phuoc 1 pineapple cooperative is the proposed pilot partner.

Goals

  • Standardise pineapple production data.
  • Track production progress and forecast supply.
  • Prepare batch traceability records that work with the authorised traceability system.

Approach

  1. Survey and baseline, data dictionary, architecture and data flows.
  2. Pilot with the cooperative, linked household groups and firms.

Expected outcomes

  • Survey and baseline reports, data dictionary and system design.
  • A pilot digital zone-management model for pineapple.
